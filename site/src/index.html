In Windows 11's own menu

Right-click an archive and BeeZip is there, in the menu Windows 11 opens first, not under Show more options. Extract it here or into its own folder, test it, or pack files into a zip, 7z or tar.zst, without opening an app.

  • A small window for each jobIt shows the progress, asks for a password when one's needed, and closes itself when all went well.
  • Asks before it overwritesWhen the archive you're making is already there, add to it, replace it, keep both, or skip.
  • Only the items you wantSettings chooses which items show, and whether they sit in a BeeZip submenu or at the top.
  • Windows 10 tooThere, BeeZip uses the classic menu, the one Windows 11 keeps under Show more options.

How the menu works. Windows 11's own menu comes with the signed installer in BeeZip 1.0.0; until then, BeeZip's menu is under Show more options.

Careful with your files

An archive can hold anything, including a path meant to land outside the folder you chose. BeeZip assumes the worst and tells you what it did.

What BeeZip checks

  • Nothing lands outside the folder you chosePaths climbing out with .. and links pointing out are skipped, and BeeZip lists them.
  • Zip bombs stop earlyAn entry that grows past 10,000 times its packed size is stopped before it can fill your disk.
  • Windows' download warning carries overFiles from a downloaded archive keep its mark, as Explorer does, so Windows still checks a program before it runs.
  • Archives aren't rewritten in placeA change writes a new archive beside the old one and swaps it in, so a failed or cancelled change leaves the original whole.
  • Encryption is never dropped quietlyAdding to an encrypted archive needs a password for the new files, and converting one needs a new password, or your say-so to decrypt it.

Change archives without starting over

Add files, delete entries and rename folders in zip, 7z, tar, cpio and ar archives. What stays is copied as it is, not compressed again. Open a file from an archive, change it, and when you come back BeeZip offers to put it back in.

The desktop app

# Add a file, into the archive's docs folder
beezip add backup.zip notes.txt --dir docs

# Rename a folder, and everything in it
beezip rename backup.zip docs papers

# See what would go, then remove it
beezip remove backup.7z "*.tmp" --dry-run

# Any archive BeeZip reads, as a 7z
beezip convert old.rar new.7z

Big archives, no waiting

The app lists an archive of a million entries without a pause, and sorts them by any column. Extracting zips, decoding xz and compressing use every core you have.

  • Browse before you extractA folder tree, the entries, and a preview of text, hex or images, in tabs.
  • Drag files straight outDrag entries onto Explorer or the desktop; they're extracted as you let go.
  • Measure it yourselfbeezip bench compares the compressors on your own files: size, speed and memory.
