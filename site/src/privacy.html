Privacy

BeeZip collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and your files never leave your computer.

Your files

BeeZip reads the archives you open and writes where you tell it to. It sends neither anywhere. Its settings are a file on your computer, config.toml.

An AI assistant you connect to beezip mcp sees what it asks BeeZip for, such as an archive's list of files or a file's text, and BeeZip writes only under the folders you allow it. What the assistant does with that is up to the assistant and the company behind it.

The network

  • The installer and the portable copy ask GitHub whether there's a newer version: when BeeZip starts, at most once a day, unless Settings turns that off, and when you click Check for updates in About. An update is downloaded from GitHub only when you click it. GitHub sees these requests as it sees any download (GitHub's privacy statement).
  • The Microsoft Store copy makes no requests of its own: the Microsoft Store updates it (Microsoft's privacy statement).
  • Linux and macOS look for updates as the installer does.

This website

The download page asks GitHub, from your browser, for the newest release, to show its files. The site has no cookies and no analytics, and GitHub Pages serves it (GitHub's privacy statement).
